About the clinic

A neighbourhood dental clinic

BR Dental Clinic has been caring for patients in Paliparan and the surrounding barangays of Dasmariñas City.

What we do

We provide general and preventive dentistry: cleaning and prophylaxis, fluoride application, fillings, extractions — including impacted molars — dentures, crowns and orthodontic treatment.

Every visit is recorded in your own patient record, so your treatment history stays with you and your dentist.

How the clinic runs

The clinic is led by Dr. Ma. Elena B. Retuerna. Appointments are booked online, reviewed by the clinic, and then confirmed. You will be notified in your account whenever an appointment is approved, rescheduled or completed.

If you need to change a visit, you can cancel or request a new time from your account without calling.
